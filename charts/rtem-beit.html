BEIT: Building Energy Intensity Toolchain

NYSERDA Global Energy & Building Hackathon · Technical Demonstration · July 2022

Third place, Technical Demonstration category · $5,000 award
NYSERDA Global Energy & Building Hackathon, hosted by Onboard Data and Building Energy Exchange · New York, 2022

Building automation systems produce far more data than most teams can act on. The gap between raw sensor streams and meaningful conclusions creates a real problem for decision-makers trying to prioritise energy conservation, maintenance, or capital investment.

BEIT closes that gap. The toolchain ingests unfiltered data from NYSERDA’s Real Time Energy Management (RTEM) database and condenses it into actionable output through a dashboard. Its modular design means individual components can be upgraded independently as methods improve.

Toolchain modules

Archetype benchmarking

Compares measured building performance against an EnergyPlus simulation of the same building type, enabling a side-by-side view of where a building falls short of its reference.

Weather normalisation

Relates monthly heating and cooling degree days to energy consumption, separating climate-driven demand from operational inefficiency.

Electrification impact estimation

Estimates the expected carbon reduction and predicted electrical load if fossil fuel systems are replaced, giving decision-makers a first-pass electrification case built from the building’s own data.

HVAC fault detection

Analyses zone temperatures, fan status, and supply–return air differentials to flag underperforming systems before they affect occupant comfort or escalate into capital failures.

Teams Energy in Cities Group, University of Victoria, BC · Intelligent Environments Lab, University of Texas at Austin
Context Participants analysed time-series data from 200+ commercial and multifamily buildings across New York State, drawn from NYSERDA’s RTEM database: building automation systems, utility meters, equipment submeters, and IoT sensors. Demo Day recap at Building Energy Exchange.
